# POL-105 Early Termination Fee

Maplewave Telecom (fictional company, synthetic demo data) | Version 2.0 | Effective 2026-01-01 | Owner: Billing Operations

## §1 The fee

When a business customer ends a contract before its end date, an early termination fee (ETF) applies:

ETF = 50% × net MRC × remaining months

- **Remaining months:** the full months after the termination month, up to and including the contract's last month.
- **When it's billed:** on the contract's final invoice, which is the invoice for the termination month.

Example: a contract ends in 2027-03 and is terminated in 2026-09. The remaining months run from 2026-10 to 2027-03, which is 6 months. With a net MRC of 400.00, the ETF is 50% × 400.00 × 6 = 1,200.00.

## §2 Waiver conditions

The ETF is waived in full only if at least one of these conditions is met:

- **(a) Chronic service failure:** in the 6 billing periods up to and including the termination month, the contract had at least 3 months where measured availability was below the SLA target (POL-102 §2 to §4). Only services with an SLA can meet this condition.
- **(b) Relocation:** the customer is moving to an address where Maplewave cannot provide the service. This must be confirmed by a serviceability check reference (an SC number).
- **(c) Upgrade:** within 30 days of termination, the customer signs a new Maplewave contract with an equal or longer term and an equal or higher net MRC.

## §3 Miscalculated fee

If the ETF that was billed differs from the §1 formula, credit the difference, even if no waiver condition is met.

## §4 Not grounds for a waiver

- General dissatisfaction with the service
- Outages that do not meet §2(a)
- Lower prices from a competitor
- The customer closing their business

Exceptions can only be made as goodwill under POL-000 §7.

## §5 Evidence

- The termination date and the final invoice ID
- Evidence for the waiver condition claimed: outage ticket IDs, the SC reference, or the new contract ID
