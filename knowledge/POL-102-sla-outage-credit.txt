# POL-102 SLA Outage Credit

Maplewave Telecom (fictional company, synthetic demo data) | Version 2.2 | Effective 2026-01-01 | Owner: Network Assurance and Billing Operations

## §1 Eligible services

SLA credits apply only to services that have an availability target in the contract (the SLA uptime %). The current targets are:

| Service | Availability target |
|---|---|
| Business Internet 500 and Business Internet 1 Gbps | 99.5% |
| SIP Trunking and Unified Communications | 99.9% |
| Dedicated Fibre 1 Gbps and Dedicated Fibre 10 Gbps | 99.95% |

The following services have no availability target and are not eligible for SLA credits:

- Business Internet 100
- Managed Wi-Fi
- Mobility Unlimited
- Mobility 20 GB

## §2 Measuring availability

Availability is measured for each affected unit (site, channel or seat), for each calendar month:

Availability % = 100 × (1 − qualifying outage minutes in the month ÷ total minutes in the month)

Add up all qualifying outages for the same contract in the same month. A 30-day month has 43,200 minutes, and a 31-day month has 44,640 minutes.

## §3 Qualifying outages

A qualifying outage is caused by the Maplewave network and is recorded in the outage records with a trouble ticket. These outages do not qualify:

- Planned maintenance announced in advance
- Outages caused by the customer's own equipment, power or premises
- Force majeure events

## §4 Credit tiers

Shortfall = SLA target − measured availability, in percentage points.

| Shortfall | Credit (share of the affected units' net MRC for that month) |
|---|---|
| 0 or less | No credit |
| More than 0, up to 0.50 | 5% |
| More than 0.50, up to 1.00 | 10% |
| More than 1.00 | 25% |

The credit is capped at 25% of the affected units' net MRC per month. Units that were not affected get no credit.

## §5 Time limit

The request must be submitted within 60 days after the outage ended. Later requests are not eligible, even if the outage qualified.

## §6 Evidence

- The outage trouble ticket ID
- The number of affected units
- The month of the outage

## §7 Worked example

A customer has SIP Trunking with 40 channels at 22.00 per channel, no contract discount and a 99.9% target. All 40 channels lost service for 300 minutes in a 30-day month.

- Availability = 100 × (1 − 300 ÷ 43,200) = 99.306%
- Shortfall = 99.9 − 99.306 = 0.594, so the 10% tier applies.
- Credit = 22.00 × 40 × 10% = 88.00
