# POL-001 Approval Authority Matrix

Maplewave Telecom (fictional company, synthetic demo data) | Version 2.4 | Effective 2026-01-01 | Owner: Finance Operations

## §1 Approval thresholds

Who must approve depends on the amount to be credited (CAD, before tax):

| Amount to be credited | Who decides |
|---|---|
| Up to 20,000.00 | Billing Adjustment Reviewer decides |
| 20,000.01 to 100,000.00 | Reviewer recommends, and a Director approves |
| Over 100,000.00 | Reviewer recommends, and a Director and the VP Finance Operations both approve |

## §2 Which amount counts

The threshold applies to the amount that will actually be credited after review (the approved amount), not the amount the account manager requested. Example: a request for 33,000.00 that is partially approved at 15,000.00 is decided by the reviewer alone.

Rejected requests and requests returned for information need no further approval. Goodwill credits always need Director approval (POL-000 §7).

## §3 Aggregation rule

Requests for the same customer and the same request type that are submitted within 90 days of each other are added together to decide which threshold applies. This prevents a large credit from being split into smaller requests to avoid Director or VP approval.

## §4 Turnaround targets

- **Reviewer:** decides within 5 business days after the request is complete (POL-000 §3).
- **Director or VP:** decides within 3 business days after the reviewer's recommendation.

Requests that miss these targets are flagged in the weekly backlog report.

## §5 Recording approvals

Each approval records the approver's name, the date and the approved amount. A credit is not applied to an invoice until every required approval has been recorded.
