# POL-103 Promotion Not Applied

Maplewave Telecom (fictional company, synthetic demo data) | Version 1.3 | Effective 2026-01-01 | Owner: Billing Operations

## §1 Scope

This policy covers a promotion recorded on the customer's contract whose monthly credit is missing, or lower than it should be, on an invoice during the promotion period.

## §2 Eligibility

A credit is due when all three conditions are met:

- (a) The promotion code is recorded on the contract.
- (b) The billing period falls within the promotion period recorded on the contract.
- (c) The invoice has no promotional credit line for that contract, or a smaller one than the contract states.

## §3 Calculation

Credit = monthly promotional credit on the contract × number of billing periods where the credit is missing.

If the credit was applied but was too small, credit the difference for that month instead.

## §4 Not eligible

- Promotions that are not recorded on the contract. A promotion cannot be added after signing through an adjustment request. The account team must use a contract amendment within the promotion's sign-up window.
- Billing periods outside the promotion period.
- Services the promotion does not cover.
